Abu Dhabi peace talks, the COP31 summit and a $64.4bn IPO led the news on 11 November 2026.

Russia, Ukraine and the US held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, the first trilateral talks since February; prisoner exchanges and security guarantees topped the agenda, Zelenskyy called the first day "constructive"; no agreement was announced (AP, BBC).

Leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, where 77 will speak; India pressed rich nations on climate-finance pledges, and the UN said current plans would cut emissions only ~6% below 2019 levels by 2035 (UNFCCC, AFP).

Anthropic set a $70-$78 range for 825 million shares in its Nasdaq IPO as ANT, valuing it at up to $1.4 trillion; Google and Nvidia will each buy $10 billion in a $26 billion placement (SEC filing, CNBC).

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts (BLS), and traders fully priced in a December Fed rate rise (Reuters).

Ebola cases in DR Congo passed 11,000, with 5,387 deaths, making it the second-largest outbreak on record; the rare Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine or treatment (Reuters).

The US Justice Department charged three men over an alleged Iran-backed plot against RAF Fairford in Britain, after fuel-laden vans were driven at the base in September (BBC). El Salvador said its forces destroyed the 400 Mawozo gang HQ in Haiti in a helicopter strike announced on Tuesday; a reported toll of about 20 is unconfirmed (BBC, Al Jazeera).

The US House voted 211-204 to send an eight-article impeachment of Defense Secretary Hegseth to committee, killing it; only Republican Thomas Massie backed it (CBS, NPR).

Six were killed and 15 injured in a mass shooting in Krakow, Poland, where citizens raised over €1.13m for injured officers. Bulgaria held a national day of mourning after Tuesday's 86-vehicle fog pile-up on the Trakia motorway killed at least 11 and injured about 50 (BBC, Reuters). A bus hit 32 vehicles in Seongnam, South Korea, injuring 57 (Yonhap).
