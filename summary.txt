Abu Dhabi talks, COP31 and a $64.4bn IPO led the news on 11 November 2026.

Russia, Ukraine and the US held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, the first since February; prisoner swaps and security guarantees topped the agenda, Zelenskyy called day one "constructive" but no deal was announced (AP, BBC).

Leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya; India pressed rich nations on finance pledges, and the UN said plans would cut emissions ~6% below 2019 levels by 2035 (UNFCCC, AFP).

Anthropic priced 825 million shares at $70-$78 in its Nasdaq IPO, worth up to $1.4 trillion; Google and Nvidia will each buy $10 billion (SEC filing, CNBC).

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts, and traders fully priced in a December Fed rate rise (BLS, Reuters).

Oil prices rose amid attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, where a tanker was struck off Oman and Iran said two more hit mines on an unauthorised route (Reuters).

DR Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases and 5,387 deaths, the second-largest on record; the Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine (Reuters).

The US Justice Department charged three men over an alleged Iran-backed plot against RAF Fairford, after fuel-laden vans were driven there in September (BBC). British police arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer near Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia; he served at NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps HQ (BBC, AP). El Salvador said it destroyed the 400 Mawozo gang HQ in Haiti in a helicopter strike announced on Tuesday, with a reported toll of about 20 unconfirmed (BBC, Al Jazeera).

The US House voted 211-204 to kill the impeachment of Defense Secretary Hegseth; only Republican Thomas Massie backed it (CBS, NPR).

Six were killed and 15 injured in a mass shooting in Krakow, where citizens raised over €1.13m. Bulgaria held a day of mourning after Tuesday's 86-vehicle fog pile-up on the Trakia motorway killed at least 11 and injured about 50 (BBC, Reuters).
